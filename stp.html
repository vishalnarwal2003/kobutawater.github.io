Information needed for sizing

  • Design occupancy / beds / rooms
  • Average and peak sewage flow
  • Inlet BOD, COD, TSS and other relevant parameters, if available
  • Required outlet standard and reuse purpose
  • Available area and underground/above-ground preference
  • Electrical supply, access and O&M expectations

Any indicative capacity must be verified by a qualified designer against site-specific data and applicable requirements.
