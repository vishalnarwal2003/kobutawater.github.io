TECHNOLOGY GUIDE

Choose a process
for the right reason.

Technology choice depends on wastewater type, design flow, load variation, footprint, energy, operator capability and required water quality.

TechnologyTypical roleKey considerations
MBBRAttached-growth biological treatmentMedia retention, aeration, screening, solids separation
SBRBatch biological treatmentCycle control, equalisation, decanting, peak-flow strategy
ASPSuspended-growth biological treatmentMLSS, sludge return, settling, aeration control
MGF / ACFParticulate and selected dissolved-organic polishingFeed quality, backwash, media change, pressure drop
UFFine suspended-solids / colloid separationMembrane fouling, backwash, CIP, pretreatment
RODissolved-solids reductionRecovery, scaling, pretreatment and reject disposal

This is a general comparison, not a design specification. Actual performance is feed-water and operating-condition dependent.

WATER REUSE

Reuse begins with
the target quality.

Flushing, irrigation, cooling-tower makeup and industrial reuse can require different quality parameters. Choose polishing and disinfection based on the actual use and applicable local standards.

Questions to answer

  • What is the intended reuse?
  • What are the required TSS, turbidity, microbial and dissolved-solids limits?
  • What is the daily reuse demand?
  • Where will backwash and RO reject go?
  • What monitoring and O&M can be provided?
