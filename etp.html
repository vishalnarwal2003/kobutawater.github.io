Build the treatment
train around the load.

Depending on the wastewater, an ETP may combine screening, oil separation, pH correction, coagulation/flocculation, clarification, biological treatment, filtration, membranes or evaporation.

Share these details

  • Daily flow (KLD) and operating hours
  • pH, COD, BOD, TSS, TDS, colour and oil & grease
  • Salinity, toxic compounds or special contaminants
  • Existing treatment steps and sludge handling
  • Required outlet limits or ZLD objective

Do not select chemicals or guarantee COD removal from COD alone. Jar tests and treatability studies may be required.
