For an initial domestic sewage estimate, choose a facility type and enter the number of rooms, beds, flats or people as applicable. The simplified assumptions are shown in the result.

Ready to estimateChoose the facility and unit count.

Simplified indicative norms: residential 135 L/person/day with 4 persons/flat; hotel 180 L/room/day; hospital 450 L/bed/day; restaurant 50 L/cover/day; school 45 L/student/day; office 45 L/employee/day. The estimator assumes 80% wastewater and 15% allowance; verify against CPHEEO/local design criteria.

03 / WATER-REUSE ROI

Estimate potential
water savings.

This simple model compares the avoided water-purchase cost against the equipment capex you enter. It is not a guaranteed payback forecast.

5.0 years (illustrative)Based on inputs shown; excludes operating expenses.

Formula: annual avoided-water cost = capacity × 365 × reuse fraction × tariff. Payback = capex ÷ annual avoided-water cost. Actual savings depend on actual reuse, uptime and operating cost.
