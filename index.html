04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose
a treatment plant.

How do I select the correct STP capacity?

Start with design occupancy and daily water use, estimate wastewater generation, then allow for peak loads and future needs. Confirm using project-specific flow data and applicable guidance.

Can treated sewage be reused?

Reuse depends on the treated water quality, intended use, local standards and any additional filtration or disinfection required. Do not assume all treated water is suitable for every application.

Can an ETP be designed for high COD wastewater?

Potentially, but the process depends on wastewater composition, biodegradability, salinity, colour, toxicity and discharge targets. A representative lab analysis and jar/bench testing may be needed.
